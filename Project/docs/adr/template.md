# ADR-NNNN - Short Decision Title

## Status

One of: Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by ADR-XXXX.

## Context

What forces the decision? Constraints, drivers (link to QA scenarios), prior ADRs, or system state that makes this decision necessary now. Two to four sentences.

## Decision

The decision itself, in one or two sentences. Active voice ("We will use X"). Be specific enough that a reader can implement it without re-deriving the choice.

## Consequences

What becomes true once this decision is in effect. Include positive consequences and operational obligations. Bullets, 3–5.

## Tradeoffs

What we give up by this decision. Bullets, 2–3. This is *not* a list of risks — it is the cost we knowingly accept.

## Rejected Alternatives

Each rejected option as its own bullet: name the option, then one line on why it was rejected. Two to three alternatives is usually enough.
